#DeveloperSundaysWithLeo
A cohort based setting with emphasis on hands-on learning.
A little back story...


After endless hours spent on tutorials and online courses, I thought I'd change it up a bit. I noticed most of my batchmates had some experience in coding in C but yet had this stigma that coding was tough and hence, I felt that I could do something to debunk that. Taking a cue from Richard Feynman, I gathered a few classmates and we started off with playing around with Python and have since come a long way, building intuitive web apps. Initially, this was a weekly thing, but with the advent of our summer break we've been developing on a daily basis.
